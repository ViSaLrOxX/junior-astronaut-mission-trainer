Sources: real vs simulated

Real NASA facts used in this game

  • MOXIE, on NASA's Perseverance rover, really made oxygen from Mars's carbon dioxide air. The O2 Generator is named after that experiment.
  • The International Space Station is protected by Earth's magnetic field. A Moon or Mars base is not, which is why shielding matters in this game.
  • Apollo 13's crew really came home safe in 1970 after an explosion, by using their lander as a lifeboat.
  • NASA's Artemis program targets the lunar south pole because shadowed craters there hold water ice.

Real recorded space-weather events

  • June 2018: a planet-wide dust storm on Mars cut sunlight so much that NASA's solar-powered Opportunity rover never woke up again. The storm in the Mars story is modelled on that event.
  • August 1972: a huge solar particle storm erupted between the Apollo 16 and 17 missions. The solar particle event in the Moon story is modelled on that kind of storm.
  • 13 March 1989: a geomagnetic storm knocked out Hydro-Québec's power grid in about 90 seconds; six million people lost power for roughly nine hours. Sources: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center and Hydro-Québec.
  • September 1859: the Carrington Event, the strongest geomagnetic storm on record, set telegraph systems sparking.

Real NASA data behind the numbers

  • NASA DONKI (Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information): the Moon story keeps a saved copy of the real May 2024 records - 6 CMEs on 8 and 9 May (the first at 05:36 UTC on 8 May), 14 solar energetic particle events from 9 to 13 May, and 5 geomagnetic storms that month, the strongest starting 10 May at 15:00 UTC (Kp 9), about 57 hours after that first CME. A saved copy, not a live feed. ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI
  • NASA Curiosity RAD: the Mars radiation scale in the game (about 0.64 mSv per day) comes from the Radiation Assessment Detector on NASA's Curiosity rover. Hassler et al., Science 2014
  • NASA MOXIE: the O2 Generator is named after the experiment that made oxygen from Mars's carbon dioxide air on NASA's Perseverance rover. NASA JPL
  • NASA GISS Mars24: a Mars sol is 24 h 39 min 35 s; used for Mars sol timing. GISS Mars24 notes
  • NASA OCHMO and NTRS: the 600 mSv career radiation dose, the ISS suit's 7-hour EVA breakdown with its 30-minute reserve, the Artemis suit's 8-hour capacity and 1-hour contingency, the 2 km/h walk-back limit and EVA buddy pairs shape the EVA planner's rules. OCHMO brief (PDF) · NTRS: Tenets of Lunar EVA
  • Not NASA, said so plainly: the Moon radiation scale (about 1.44 mSv per day) is from the Chang'e-4 Lunar Lander Neutrons and Dosimetry instrument - a Chinese lander carrying a German instrument, not a NASA one. Zhang et al., Science Advances 2020

Simulated for learning

Every number in the mission - oxygen, water, food, power, parts, health, morale, budgets and dates - is a simplified teaching model, not real telemetry. Mission lengths and costs are not NASA figures. Messages from Earth arrive at scheduled windows: a training-game rule, not a claim that Moon messages take a day to arrive.
